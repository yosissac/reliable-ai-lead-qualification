# Community Conduct

Participation should be professional, respectful, and evidence-oriented.

- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing experience levels.
- Do not publish personal data, credentials, private client material, or
  security-sensitive details.
- Distinguish measured results from inference and opinion.
- Give credit for prior work and contributions.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed
to protect contributors, users, or the project. Serious conduct concerns should
be reported privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
